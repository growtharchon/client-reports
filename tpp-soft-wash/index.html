Executive Summary

  1. Strong technical foundation, weak growth levers. The website scores well on technical SEO (structured data, title tags, mobile UX, dedicated service pages) but is missing the high-impact growth drivers that move the needle in the Local Pack: paid ads, review velocity, and GBP optimization.
  2. No paid visibility at all. TPP Soft Wash runs zero Google Ads or Local Services Ads. Competitors who launch LSAs will capture high-intent leads above all organic results. This is the single biggest missed opportunity.
  3. Review profile is fragile. Seven reviews — all posted in a single burst in June 2026 — signal a batch pattern rather than genuine ongoing customer feedback. Sustained review velocity (2–4 per month) is critical for Local Pack competitiveness and consumer trust.

#03 Google Ads / LSAs Fail
Evidence
Searched "pressure washing saline mi" — no Local Services Ads (LSAs) and no Google Search Ads showing for this keyword. TPP Soft Wash is not running any paid ads. Reviewers: Brandon West, Carlee Gulliver, Joseph Galura, kathaza akter, Chelsea Respondek, Lauren Jakubowski, Ken Jakubowski. No new reviews since the initial burst.
