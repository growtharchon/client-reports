01 / Finding

What the evidence supports

High confidence
✓

The written revisions were implemented visually.

R1 landed across August 5 and 13. R2 landed across August 17 and 18. Requested removals were hidden from visitors before dormant source was deleted later.

!

Implementation was not converted into trusted acceptance.

Feedback was fragmented, Loom was missed, completion messages lacked a versioned checklist, and no formal independent content/design approval gate was recorded.

“We implemented the written revisions in the visitor-facing site, but our process for consolidating, checking, confirming and approving that work was not strong enough.”

06 / Root causes

Six controls that were missing

01

No authoritative ledger

Dropbox, documents, Loom and email were never reconciled into one acceptance record.

02

Unqualified completion language

“Ready” and “implemented” were not tied to a version, evidence or exceptions.

03

QA versus acceptance

Strong technical QA was not paired with a final independent design/content gate.

04

No explicit sign-off

Positive feedback on features and pages was treated too close to final release approval.

05

Distributed ownership

No single owner reconciled assets, code, tracking, client feedback and release status.

06

Invisible SEO value

Completed SEO and measurement work was not packaged into a client-readable status report.
