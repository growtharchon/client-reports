The landing pages are not duplicate content

Prepared for Eric Millinger Outrider Digital 29 July 2026 21 of 21 pages reviewed

No action required

We compared all 21 landing pages against their closest equivalents on the main site. They are not duplicates, and we recommend leaving them exactly as they are. Redirecting or removing them would break the Google Ads campaigns running to them — a real cost, to solve a problem that isn't occurring.

Conclusions

  • The pages are not duplicates. Across all 21 pairs, phrase-level overlap with the matching main-site page runs between 3.8% and 5.5%.
  • For context, two unrelated pages on the main site itself overlap by 37% — roughly seven times more than any landing page overlaps with the main site. If 4% were a duplicate content problem, the main site would have a far larger one internally.
  • The overlap that does exist is unavoidable shared fact — ISO 9001, the Monterrey facility, USMCA tariff treatment, tolerances, the company name. Two pages describing the same certified business will always share this material.
  • The two properties were built for opposite jobs — the landing pages convert paid ad clicks, the main site earns organic rankings. They differ in length, structure, navigation and conversion path, as the comparison below shows.
  • The landing pages are set to noindex, so they are not eligible to appear in Google's organic results and cannot compete with the main site for rankings.
