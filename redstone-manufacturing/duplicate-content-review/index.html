The landing pages are not duplicate content

Prepared for Eric Millinger Outrider Digital 29 July 2026 21 of 21 pages reviewed

No action required

We compared all 21 landing pages against their closest equivalents on the main site. They are not duplicates, and we recommend leaving them exactly as they are. Redirecting or removing them would break the Google Ads campaigns running to them — a real cost, to solve a problem that isn't occurring.

Conclusions

  • The pages are not duplicates. Across all 21 pairs, phrase-level overlap with the matching main-site page runs between 3.8% and 5.5%, and sequence similarity between 10.2% and 14.0%. Every pair passed.
  • The small overlap that does exist is unavoidable shared fact — ISO 9001, the Monterrey facility, USMCA tariff treatment, tolerances, the company name. Any two pages describing the same certified business will share this material. Beyond it, the pages have little in common: the landing pages run roughly half the length of their main-site counterparts and are built for a different job entirely.
