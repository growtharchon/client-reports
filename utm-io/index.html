What this is not

This isn't a measurement artefact. Organic Social sessions fell from 1,201 in the week of 22 Dec 2025 to 71 in the week of 5 Jan 2026 and have averaged under 20/week ever since. That is a switch being thrown — a paused posting cadence, a retired scheduler, or a tracking change on social links. It's worth finding, because it's the largest single movement in the account and it has nothing to do with search.

Email did the same: 2,930 sessions in the week of 1 Dec 2025 to under 60/week from January.

§ 03

Why April→July looks like a slide

Two things are happening at once, and only one of them is a problem.

First: this account has a summer. In 2025, web.utm.io ran 65.0 clicks/day in April down to 47.5 in July — a 27% seasonal fall. In 2026 the combined footprint ran 120.4 in April down to 91.4 in July — a 24% fall. Near-identical seasonal shape, at roughly double the volume. The April peak was never the new baseline; it was the top of the annual curve.

Second: the impression pool is shrinking. Comparing the same 31-day window in both years on web.utm.io:

25 Jun – 25 Jul20252026ChangeReading
Clicks1,4681,430−3%Held flat.
Impressions715,668420,845−41%Google is showing the pages to far fewer people.

That combination — impressions collapsing, positions improving, CTR climbing, clicks flat — is the signature of AI Overviews absorbing informational queries. The pages didn't get worse; the SERP got smaller. It shows up most sharply on the pure how-to content: /blog/track-linkedin-ads-with-utms/ went from position 10.4 to 7.5 and still lost 74% of its clicks. Ranking better is no longer sufficient on that class of query.

The strategic consequence

Tool and transactional pages are not affected the same way — someone searching “utm builder” wants to use a builder, and an AI answer doesn't satisfy that. Every page that grew in this project is a tool page or a page with a tool intent; almost every page that shrank is an explainer. That is where the next phase of budget belongs.

Read the utmbuilder.com column carefully

That property's Search Console history only starts 11 Dec 2025, so its “before” figures cover 17 days measured against 175 days after. The raw counts are real but the ratio is not: on a per-day basis, “utm builder” on utmbuilder.com went from 17.4 to 23.1 clicks/day — a genuine +33%, not the 14× the raw numbers suggest. The web.utm.io column is a like-for-like 180 days against 175.

The cannibalisation risk we flagged at kickoff did materialise, and it was worth it. We warned that pushing utmbuilder.com on “utm builder” would cost web.utm.io some of the same term. It did: web.utm.io lost 195 clicks on that query — a like-for-like fall of 48%. utmbuilder.com picked up 3,750 clicks on the same term over the shorter measured window, and even after normalising the windows its daily rate on it rose a third. The trade was clearly positive. The same pattern holds on “linkedin utm builder” and “utm generator”.

One number to watch that isn't ours to fix: the brand query. “utm.io” improved from position 9.2 to 2.6 — a large ranking win — and clicks still fell from 512 to 440. When you rank 2.6 for your own name and clicks decline, the constraint is how many people are searching the brand, not where you sit. That tracks with Direct traffic, which is down from 1,368 to 852 sessions/day over the same period. Both are demand-side, and both sit outside an SEO scope.

§ 07

What we'd tell you if you weren't paying us

The parts of this that are genuinely soft.

  1. utmbuilder.com peaked in March and gave most of it back

    47.6 clicks/day in December, 78.7 at the March peak, 45.5 now. Some of that is the same summer seasonality that hit web.utm.io, but the fall is steeper than season alone accounts for (−42% vs. −27% on the sister site). The position on “utm builder” only moved from 4.8 to 4.5 — we did not get it to position 2, and the supporting pages haven't yet had enough link equity to force it. This is the single clearest piece of unfinished business.

  2. Backlink delivery isn't verifiable from this data

    Search Console and GA4 don't report referring domains, and the Semrush API quota on this account is exhausted as of today, so we can't put a number on the 10–15 links here. What is observable is that the specific pages we allocated links to are the ones whose positions moved most: UTM Best Practices 19.5→7.9, the QR generator 43.7→9.3, the homepage 20.8→10.8, utmbuilder.com 11.0→8.1. We'll send the actual link log separately rather than infer it.

  3. Six of eighteen optimisations are flat or down

    UTM Campaign Tracking, Conventions, Link Builder and UTM Templates all moved sideways or slightly backwards. On the first three, positions worsened — those are real misses, not AI Overview effects. On the informational pages that lost clicks while holding position (UTM Cleaner, LinkedIn Ads, Naming Conventions) the cause is the SERP, but the outcome is the same for you.

  4. Your biggest channel is the one nobody is working on

    Direct traffic is 5–6× organic search on this property and it's down roughly a third year on year, on top of the social and email shutdown. Organic search is currently the only channel in the account that is growing. That's a good thing for this engagement and a bad thing for the business, and it deserves a conversation that isn't about SEO.

§ 08

What we'd do next

In priority order, based on what the data says is working.

  1. Find the January switch

    Before anything else. Organic Social and Email both went to near-zero in the same week. If that's a tracking break, the reported decline is partly fictional and fixing it changes the picture immediately. If it's a real pause in posting and sending, restarting it is the single highest-leverage thing available — it was worth 260 sessions/day.

  2. Move budget from explainers to tools

    Every winner in this project has tool intent. Every loser is an explainer competing with an AI answer. The next content batch should be free micro-tools with a page around them — validator, bulk builder, parameter linter — not more “what is” posts.

  3. Finish utmbuilder.com

    It's at position 4.5 for the money term with six supporting pages already live and indexed. Concentrated link acquisition on the homepage plus internal linking from the six supporting pages is the shortest path to position 2–3, and it's the highest-commercial-intent traffic in the account.

  4. Publish the two missing pieces

    GCLID vs UTM and UTM Parameters for Bing. Both queries are already improving on unrelated pages, which usually means a dedicated page ranks fast.

  5. Report on search and social separately

    The blended organic view is what caused this conversation. Split it. We're rebuilding the dashboard view for you either way — see below.

§ 09

Our own error, for the record

Two configuration faults on our side that meant our dashboard was under-reporting this account.

While pulling this data we found that our reporting dashboard had UTM.io connected to the wrong Search Console property. It was set to http://utm.io/ — a URL-prefix property that matches only that exact scheme and host, and carries 5,212 lifetime clicks. The actual footprint is https://web.utm.io/ (22,257) plus sc-domain:utmbuilder.com (14,530). Our dashboard was seeing roughly 12% of your organic search traffic.

Separately, the account was archived in our system on 7 May 2026, which stopped the nightly sync entirely — so even the 12% stopped updating in early May. Neither fault affects any number in this document: everything here is pulled directly from the Google APIs against the correct properties. But it does mean the dashboard you were sent was wrong, and we're fixing both.
